## 📌 Project Overview

The **Climate & Agriculture Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze the relationship between **climate conditions and agricultural productivity** across different countries, regions, years, and crop types.

The dashboard transforms raw agricultural and environmental data into interactive visualizations and actionable insights.

The analysis focuses on:

- 🌡️ Temperature
- 🌾 Crop Yield
- 💧 Irrigation Access
- 🌪️ Extreme Weather Events
- 🌫️ CO₂ Emissions
- 🌍 Regional & Country-level Performance
- 🌱 Crop Type Distribution

The goal is to understand how environmental and climate-related factors influence agricultural productivity and identify patterns that can support **data-driven decision-making**.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the relationship between temperature and crop yield.
- Study the impact of extreme weather events on agricultural productivity.
- Compare crop yield and irrigation access across countries.
- Analyze CO₂ emissions across different regions.
- Identify yearly crop yield growth patterns.
- Compare agricultural performance across crop types.
- Build an interactive dashboard for exploratory analysis.
- Present complex datasets through clear and meaningful visualizations.

# 📈 Key Insights

### 🌡️ Temperature & Crop Yield

The dashboard highlights variations in crop yield across different temperature conditions and regions.

### 💧 Irrigation Access

Countries with better irrigation access can maintain comparatively stable agricultural productivity despite environmental variations.

### 🌪️ Extreme Weather Events

Extreme weather events such as floods and droughts represent significant risks to agricultural production.

### 🌫️ CO₂ Emissions

Regional differences in CO₂ emissions provide additional environmental context when analyzing agricultural performance.

### 🌾 Crop Diversity

Different crop types show different levels of representation and performance, highlighting the importance of crop diversification.

### 🌍 Regional Differences

Agricultural performance varies significantly across Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania, and other regions.
